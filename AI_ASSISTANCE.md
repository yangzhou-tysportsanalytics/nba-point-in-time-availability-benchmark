# AI assistance and what it changed

AI agents (Anthropic Claude models, run by the authors through a coding-agent interface, September 2026) were used throughout this project: they wrote analysis code to written plans, drafted text that the authors edited, and audited both. The authors chose every specification and fixed every decision rule before the corresponding analysis was run. Numbers were checked against the result files by an agent audit and by the authors; that process found stale numbers twice, most recently a figure left in the conclusion after the analysis behind it had been amended, so we describe it as a repeated check rather than a guarantee. No agent chose a specification, changed a decision rule, or decided what to claim. Agents also do not replace the human audit of the data (the paper).

Because several of this paper's claims changed in response to agent audits, The table below lists each audit and what it changed, including the findings we had to act on. The reports themselves are in the repository.
